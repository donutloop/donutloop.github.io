# ADR 0019 — Construction sites with animated tower cranes

Date: 2026-02-26

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The city skyline was static: every corner lot was a finished building, and the
only motion came from cars, pedestrians, and aircraft. A "best procedural city
simulation" should feel alive — including places where the city is still being
built. Tower cranes (rotating jib + hoisting hook) add vertical interest and a
growing-city narrative, and a deterministic animation makes them verifiable.

## Decision

Add a `ConstructionSystem` (new `src/construction.js`) and generate active
construction sites in `createCityChunk`:

- Some corner lots become construction sites instead of finished buildings: a
  partially-built concrete core, a dark-metal scaffolding frame around the
  footprint, and a tower crane (mast + rotating jib + counterweight + hoisted
  hook block + cable).
- Construction placement is **deterministic**: it uses `SimplexNoise` on the
  lot's world coordinates, plus a rule that the origin chunk always carries one
  site so the verification harness has a guaranteed crane.
- Cranes are **regular (non-instanced) meshes** (not merged into the chunk
  `InstancedMesh`) because their matrices must animate per-frame.
- `ConstructionSystem.update(delta)` swings the jib (`phase += delta * SPEED`)
  and hoists/slides the hook block deterministically — the animation is
  reproducible for `check_world.mjs`.
- The chunk carries `chunkData.construction` records; `ChunkManager` hands them
  to `ConstructionSystem.loadChunk(cx, cz, chunkData)` and drops them on
  `unloadChunk`. `main.js` calls `constructionSystem.update(delta)` each frame.

## Consequences

- The skyline gains living, animated construction (a genuinely new city system).
- Verification is deterministic: origin chunk carries a crane, `craneCount()`
  matches loaded sites, and jib rotation advances by `delta * SPEED`.
- Cranes are removed with their chunk on unload, so memory is bounded by the
  visible streamed area.
- Construction sites push a collider so traffic/pedestrians avoid the fenced lot.
